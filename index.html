01

The studio

A small team with a narrow focus. We do not chase genres; we build one kind of game very well and refine it across releases.

01 / Discipline

Design before code

Every mechanic is specified in writing before an engineer touches it. If a rule cannot be stated simply, it is not ready to be built.

02 / Evidence

Measure, then decide

Prototypes ship with instrumentation from the first build. We tune difficulty from player data rather than from internal opinion.

03 / Longevity

Support after launch

Released titles keep receiving compatibility and stability work. A game is not finished on the day it appears in a store.

03

Operating principles

Four rules that constrain every product decision we make.

01

No manufactured waiting

We do not add timers, energy meters or cooldowns to games that do not need them. Friction is a design failure, not a monetisation tool.

02

Clear pricing

Any purchase is priced and described before the button is pressed. Rewarded advertising is offered as a choice, never imposed as a gate.

03

Minimal data

We collect only what is required to run and improve the game. The Privacy Policy states this without legal padding.

04

Fix before feature

Known defects are resolved ahead of new content. A crash is not a roadmap item; it is a broken product.
